# Ideas for Kero Learns Japanese videos

Want Kero to learn a word? Got a story for a Short or an episode? Share it here.

**[Share an idea](../../issues/new/choose)**

Every idea here also shows on **[Kero's ideas page](https://kerolearnsjapanese.com/ideas/)**, where anyone can tap a heart to back it.

Ideas for **KEROTOWN**, the game, and game bugs go in the **[KEROTOWN tracker](https://github.com/Kero-Learns-Japanese/issue-tracker)** instead.

You need a GitHub account to post here. GitHub accounts are for people aged 13 and over. If you are younger, comment your idea under any Kero video on YouTube instead, and it can still go on the list.

## Keep yourself safe

Everything you post here can be seen by anyone on the internet.

- Never post your real name, your age, your school, where you live, your phone number or your email address.
- Never post a password.
- It is fine to use the name you use on YouTube.

Posts that break these rules, or that are unkind, are taken down.

## What makes a good idea

- **A word:** the word in English, and the Japanese if you know it. A funny mix-up helps too, like なす (nasu, aubergine) sounding like NASA.
- **A video idea:** what happens in it, in a sentence or two.
- One idea a post is best, so each one can be made on its own.

## Find ideas

Each idea has a label for what kind it is and one for where it is up to:

| Label | Means |
|---|---|
| `word` | A word for Kero to learn |
| `story` | A story or a video idea |
| `language` | Kero (or a friend) learning another language |
| `to do` | On Kero's list |
| `being made` | Kero is making it now |
| `maybe later` | Not for now |
| `done` | The video is out (the issue is closed) |

Click a label to see only those ideas, or use these:

- [Words on the list](../../issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3Aword+label%3A%22to+do%22)
- [Video ideas on the list](../../issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3Astory+label%3A%22to+do%22)
- [Being made](../../issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3A%22being+made%22)
- [Done](../../issues?q=is%3Aclosed+label%3Adone)

Give an idea a 👍 if you want it too.

## What happens next

We read every idea. An idea gets a label once it is on Kero's list, and from then on it shows on the ideas page. When its video comes out, it is marked `done` and closed.
